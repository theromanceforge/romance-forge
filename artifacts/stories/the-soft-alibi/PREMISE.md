## Central secret (mystery engine)

**What the building kept:** Vivienne’s presence was always a schedule more than a marriage. Nolan’s money and Pell’s silence managed the optics. [player_name] was invited into the gaps — and may have seen a night, a bag, a conversation, a bruise of absence she chose not to name.

**Ambiguity lock (critical):** Early/mid layers must **NOT** settle whether Vivienne left willingly, was paid to vanish, is dead, or is staging her own absence. The **cadence of thinning visits** is the dread engine (weekly → monthly → gone). Nolan may be guilty, protective, or both. Brooks stays **missing-persons through L1–4**; **homicide interest escalates L5+** without requiring a body. Do **not** write “Vivienne is dead” or “Vivienne ran” as settled fact in Layers 1–4; all four fates (willing leave / paid vanish / dead / staging) stay open into L10; drip calendar gaps, perfume, Rhea’s log, Pell’s NDAs, Nolan’s half-truths.

**Truth architecture (payoff Layers 8–10):** Late layers earn confirmation / denial / twist — **all four fates remain L10-open:** leave willingly / paid vanish / dead / staging. Endings differ by *truth vs protection*, *Nolan exposed vs bargained*, *testify / bury / burn the alibi*, *Brooks justice vs private peace*, *Vivienne named among the missing vs returned vs confirmed gone*. Secrets plant early (calendar cadence, perfume, cufflink tick, Rhea’s eyes, something [player_name] saw) and pay off late.
