## Who this story is

[player_name]’s younger sister vanished years ago. The case went cold. She kept something — a detail, a lie, a scrap of evidence — that she told herself was protection. Now **unidentified remains** upriver reopen old files: a charm *like* Renny’s is found, but **no confirmation** that the remains are Renny. Dread and hope fight it out. **Detective William Akers** (mid-30s) catches the revival. [player_name] is mid-20s — the gap is small, the pressure is not. Attraction is immediate and dangerous: he needs her cooperation; she needs truth without destroying what’s left of her family — and without letting anyone bury hope too early. The romance is trust under interrogation lights. The mystery is what she kept, whether Renny is among the dead, and who benefits from either answer.

## Hook

Seven years after Renny — then a teenager — disappeared from a pier-side festival, a construction crew finds **unidentified partial remains** upriver. Among the debris: a silver charm that *could* match Renny’s missing bracelet piece. Lab ID is pending; rumor is faster than science. William Akers (mid-30s) pulls the cold file. [player_name] (mid-20s) is listed as the familial contact who “withheld nonessential detail.” He shows up at her door with a warrant for records and a look that says he knows she kept something — not that he knows Renny is dead. **Hope and dread share the doorway.** She can cooperate, lawyer up, or lie again. The quiet she built starts to crack. The question *is it her?* must **linger deep** into mid/late layers — do not resolve early.

## Central secret (mystery engine)

**What she kept:** On the night Renny vanished, [player_name] saw **Owen** near the pier with someone who wasn’t supposed to be there — and she accepted Owen’s story that Renny had “run off with friends” because admitting otherwise would have blown the family apart (and because Owen implied Renny’s trouble was drugs/debt [player_name] didn’t want on the record). She never put Owen at the scene in her official statement.

**Ambiguity lock (critical):** Early/mid layers treat the upriver remains as **possibly Renny, possibly not**. Forensics drip (timing, age range, charm probability) fuels dread/hope without a hard ID until **late** (Layer 8–10 territory unless a branch earns an earlier partial). Missing-child presence — Renny as absence — must linger; do not write “the body is Renny” as settled fact in L1–4.

**Murder / disappearance truth (payoff Layers 8–10):** Someone wanted Renny silent about a **harbor smuggling / evidence-tampering chain** touching a mid-level official and Owen’s desperation. Whether the upriver remains are hers is part of the late reveal architecture (confirm / deny / twist). Will’s investigation and [player_name]’s confession collide; the romance is whether he can love the woman who obstructed her own sister’s case — and whether she can trust a badge after the system failed Renny once.

Secrets plant early (charm-without-certainty, festival night, Owen’s alibi crack) and pay off late. Endings differ by *truth vs protection*, *Will vs leaving*, *Owen exposed vs bargained*, *public justice vs private peace*, *Renny named among the dead vs Renny still missing*.

## Romantic axis

William Akers (mid-30s) is competent, sleep-deprived, and allergic to being lied to — and he is **not** a clean-cut procedural hero. He carries a **real dark past**, live secrets, and **brutal charm**: the kind of sharpness that feels like a weapon and a wound. Attraction should taste dangerous. Plant hints early (scars in the voice, a file he won’t open, a joke that lands like a threat); do **not** dump his whole past in Layers 1–4 — the cold case (and she) will scrape it raw later. [player_name] is mid-20s — close enough to burn, far enough that the badge still feels like a different country. He wants the sister as a witness and, against his better judgment, as a person. She wants answers more than absolution — until absolution becomes the only way she can stay in his bed without tasting betrayal. Attraction is heat under procedural pressure; **trust is the real plot**. No second LI in v1.

Branching intent (early layers):

- **scene2a (precinct):** Lead with Will and the case file — cooperation under fluorescent light.  
- **scene2b (home / pier):** Lead with memory and the kept detail — family pressure before the badge.  
- Layers 3–4: how much she admits; whether she lets Will into Renny’s room; whether Owen is confronted or shadowed.  
- Layers 5–7: forensics that *almost* ID the remains, festival witnesses, charm probability, Will/MC intimacy escalating with stakes — Renny’s fate still contested.  
- Layers 8–9: irreversible choices (testify / bury / burn Owen / leak to press).

## William Akers — buried teeth (author lock)

**The wound (specific):** Five years ago Will ran a harbor warrant with an ex-partner, **Detective Nina Solis**. He pushed the entry early to protect a CI. The raid went sideways. A teenager in the wrong hallway caught a bullet — not from Will’s gun, but from the chaos he ordered. Nina took the official fall; Will kept his badge and a sealed IA file he still won’t open. He wears brutal charm like armor over that night: jokes that cut, tenderness that feels like a threat, allergy to lies because his own career is built on one careful silence.

**Plant early (L1–4):** cracked knuckles / a flinch at “partner” / refusal to discuss Solis / a joke about “kids in hallways” that dies mid-breath / Ellison’s side-eye when his methods get sharp.  
**Pay off late (L7–10):** Solis returns, the sealed file leaks, or the Renny case rhymes with that hallway — his charm scrapes raw against [player_name]’s kept secret.

## Craft bar (can’t-close-the-tab)

1. Every scene ends on a **hook** — unfinished kiss, half-spoken truth, knock, evidence face-up.  
2. Choice labels = **verbs of consequence** (risk / loyalty / desire / justice).  
3. **Hot** = body-POV rewrite; one physical through-line; heat advances trust/plot — not Warm+garnish.  
4. Rotate anchors; cut elegant-absolute stacks; dialogue with subtext.  
5. Exits **hurt to leave** — unanswered question + bodily want.  
6. Vulgar language earned in Hot; Warm blunt OK, not porn-diction by default.  
7. Murder-romance line: grief and sex can coexist; do not eroticize the corpse or the kill.
